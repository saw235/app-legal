Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

This application is a personal content-publishing tool used solely by its owner to publish the owner's own video content to the owner's own social media accounts.

Data we collect

None from third parties. The application has no public users, no sign-up, and no tracking. The only data processed is the owner's own video files and captions, transmitted directly to the destination platform's API (e.g. TikTok, Instagram) for publishing.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party beyond the destination platforms the owner publishes to.

Data retention

Access tokens are stored encrypted in the owner's private automation system and can be revoked at any time from the respective platform's settings.
